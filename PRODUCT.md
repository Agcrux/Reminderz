# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two groups. The organizer (Anyelo Cruz) picks their free times for one Monday–Sunday week, adds a Zoom link, and shares a single booking link. Guests are the people the organizer meets with: they open the link, usually once and on any device, pick a time, enter their name and email, and are booked on the spot. The organizer comes back to a private dashboard to see who booked which time.

## Product Purpose

Reminderz replaces back-and-forth scheduling. The organizer publishes their week, and guests book themselves into any open time. A booked time stays open, so several guests can join the same Zoom call. Success is a guest going from the link to a confirmed booking, Zoom link in hand, in under a minute, without the organizer having to confirm anything.

## Brand Personality

Professional and calm. Polished and quiet, like booking time with a consultant or advisor. Short, plain wording with no hype, exclamation marks or decorative flourishes. Three words: composed, clear, dependable.

## Anti-references

- Decorative display type in the interface, such as the italic serif accent headline in the first version, which the organizer found weird.
- Oversized marketing-style hero headings, and tiny all-caps labels above every section.
- Chatty or cutesy scheduling copy ("Let's find a time that works!").

## Design Principles

1. The task is the page: a guest sees the open times and the booking form without scrolling past decoration.
2. Plain words over clever ones: every label and message says exactly what happens next.
3. Calm confidence: restrained color and one well-tuned typeface; emphasis comes from weight and spacing, not ornament.
4. Nothing left to confirm: booking is instant, so the interface states outcomes (booked, Zoom link, emailed) instead of asking for follow-up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: at least 4.5:1 contrast for body and placeholder text, 16px minimum for body and input text, visible focus states, full keyboard use, and layouts that hold up at 200% zoom and at phone widths. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
